Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 21, 2026

Track Record (musicadata.com) is an independent data project owned and operated by Longo Co. LLC. This Privacy Policy outlines how our website collects, uses, and safeguards information when you browse our music data charts.

1. Information We Automatically Collect

Like most websites, we may utilize third-party analytics and advertising tools (such as Google Analytics and Google AdSense) to analyze visitor traffic patterns. These tools automatically collect certain non-personal information, including your IP address, browser type, referring pages, and the time/date of your visit.

2. Google AdSense & Cookies

Google uses cookies to serve ads on musicadata.com. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve targeted ads to our users based on their visit to our site and other locations across the Internet.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website.
  • Users may choose to opt out of personalized advertising at any time by visiting the official Google Ads Settings page.

3. Third-Party Web Hosting

Our website files are securely hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect basic server log data from visitors (such as IP addresses) to maintain platform security and ensure service reliability.
